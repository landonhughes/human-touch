# Final Review

Use this as a final visual QA pass.

## 1. Intent

For every prominent element, ask:

> Why is this here?

Good answers can be:

- functional
- narrative
- compositional
- stylistic
- emotional
- brand-related
- contextual

"Because it looks cool" can be acceptable when the creative concept intentionally prioritizes spectacle.
The issue is accidental decoration, not decoration itself.

## 2. Structural pass

Check people, objects, environments, websites, interfaces, and video for:

- anatomy
- geometry
- attachment points
- perspective
- scale
- reflections
- shadows
- repeating artifacts
- merged objects
- malformed labels
- impossible construction
- broken responsive layouts
- inconsistent states
- continuity errors
- changing product details
- 3D normals, intersections, material scale, and topology appropriate to use
- pixel-grid consistency, readable clusters, palette, and sprite registration

For 3D deliverables, review the asset as well as its render where available. For
pixel art, inspect native-size readability as well as enlarged edges. Do not infer
export readiness or a strict pixel grid from presentation alone.

## 3. Physical plausibility

Where realism is expected, check:

- gravity
- balance
- weight
- contact points
- perspective
- light direction
- material behavior

Stylized work may intentionally bend these rules, but the bending should feel consistent.

## 4. AI-tell pass

Look for accidental:

- generic polish
- meaningless micro-detail
- waxy texture
- glowing edges
- strange geometry
- symmetry
- generic faces
- particles
- excessive depth of field
- random decorations
- implausible lighting
- repeating patterns
- texture melting
- arbitrary complexity
- card-grid website layouts
- generated-video camera drift
- shot-to-shot identity changes

Do not remove intentional versions of these.

## 5. Composition and pacing pass

Check:

- focal point
- reading order
- hierarchy
- balance
- quiet areas
- density
- negative space
- stylistic consistency
- section rhythm for websites
- shot rhythm for video
- transition logic
- continuity

## 6. Compatibility pass

Before revising, confirm:

- Does this change conflict with the user's explicit request?
- Does it conflict with the active brand system?
- Does it conflict with a specialized image-generation, web, video, or design skill?
- Is the suspected issue actually an intentional stylistic choice?

If yes, preserve the intentional direction.

## Success criterion

The final result should feel deliberate rather than default-generated.
