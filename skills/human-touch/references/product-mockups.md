# Product Mockups

## Goal

Create product imagery that looks intentionally art-directed for the actual product and market.

## Do not force ordinary products into luxury-ad mode

When no stronger direction exists, prefer believable:

- environments
- props
- lighting
- camera angles
- material treatment
- packaging dimensions

If luxury, cinematic, surreal, or highly polished art direction is explicitly requested,
lean into it while preserving physical and structural coherence.

## Props

Props should:

- establish context
- explain scale
- communicate usage
- reinforce brand character
- balance composition

Remove arbitrary props that exist only to fill space.

## Construction

Verify:

- dimensions
- cap or lid attachment
- labels
- seams
- folds
- edges
- handles
- thickness
- shadows
- reflections

Packaging should look manufacturable unless intentionally fantastical.

## Prompt pattern

Create a realistic or deliberately stylized mockup of [THING] in [CONTEXT].
Preserve the requested brand and visual direction. Use [LIGHT SOURCE],
[CAMERA POSITION], and [MATERIALS]. Include props only when they support
context, scale, usage, or composition. Avoid accidental generated-image
clichés that are not part of the requested art direction.

When a mockup includes an actual 3D asset, also read [3D Modeling](3d-modeling.md)
for geometry, material, scale, and delivery checks.
