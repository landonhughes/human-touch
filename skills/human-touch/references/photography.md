# Photography

## Goal

Create imagery that feels deliberately photographed rather than statistically beautified.

## Respect explicit style

If the user intentionally requests editorial, cinematic, flash, surreal, luxury,
experimental, or fashion photography, preserve that direction.

This reference mainly prevents accidental photographic clichés.

## Lighting

Choose or infer an actual lighting setup.

Examples:

- north-facing window
- cloudy daylight
- fluorescent office lighting
- grocery-store overhead lighting
- tungsten table lamp
- late-afternoon sun
- direct phone flash
- studio softbox
- deliberately theatrical multi-light setup

Lighting should either make physical sense or clearly function as intentional stylization.

## Camera

Camera choices should have a reason.

Default toward believable perspective when no direction is provided.

Possible choices:

- eye-level documentary framing
- product tabletop framing
- portrait lens
- ultrawide environmental shot
- deliberate low angle
- deliberate fisheye
- deliberate shallow depth of field

Extreme choices are valid when intentional.

## Materials

Different materials must behave differently.

- wood should not casually reflect like glass
- plastic should not behave like metal
- fabric needs thickness and believable folds
- paper bends differently from cloth
- glass reveals and distorts what is behind it
- metal reflects its environment
- skin should retain natural texture unless stylized otherwise

## Imperfection

Use contextual imperfection when it supports realism.

Examples:

- slight wrinkles
- minor wear
- fingerprints
- subtly uneven placement
- natural posture
- environmental clutter
- imperfect hair placement

Do not add grime or scratches merely to signal authenticity.

## Physical pass

Check:

- gravity
- balance
- perspective
- shadows
- reflections
- object scale
- contact points
- anatomy
- environmental consistency
