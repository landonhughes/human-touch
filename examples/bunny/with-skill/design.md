# Carrot intermission

A little stage trick interrupts a snack, then the snack resumes.

- One cream bunny, one orange carrot, one plum magician hat, on a warm blush background.
- Palette: background #f7ece4, foreground #4d3b55, fur #fff9e9, ear pink #eeb8b1, carrot #ea8849, leaf #648b69, ribbon #bc8793.
- Rounded 2D cutout shapes with a controlled 4 px plum outline and warm contact shadows. No words, decorative particles, or camera motion.
- Bunny and hat are the two focal objects. The static oval floor establishes contact and a grounded stage; a simple background circle frames the action.
- No typography is needed because the requested story is purely visual.
- Shared across both variants. Baseline uses smooth direct jumps; Human Touch adds anticipation, asymmetric ears, flight stretch, compression on landing, and purposeful pauses.

## Motion references
Hyperframes animation rules: svg-icon-enrichment (explicit SVG pivots), sine-wave-loop (bounded chew phase only), and press-release-spring (adapted anticipation/recovery).
